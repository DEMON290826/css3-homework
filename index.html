Start on foot

Ann Arbor is a small city that rewards walking. The Huron River bends through the middle of town, the university sits on the eastern hill, and most neighborhoods keep a park within a ten minute stroll. This small site collects a few of the routes I take when I want an hour outside.

Each route below is flat, paved, and lit in the evening. None of them require a car, a permit, or a reservation. Bring water in July and a warm layer in October.

What you will find here

  • Parks — the green spaces that anchor each walk.
  • Walks — distances, surfaces, and the best hour to go.
  • Access — where the curb cuts and benches are.

The pictures on this site are described in text so that the routes still make sense with images turned off. If you notice a barrier on any of these paths, the city takes reports through its parks office.
